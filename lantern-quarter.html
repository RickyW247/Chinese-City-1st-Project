The quarter

The Quarter lost the argument about power in 2049 and won every argument about shape since.

When the valley floor was rewired, the utility offered the Lantern Quarter a subsidy to convert: sealed panels, uniform output, a colour temperature chosen in a room nine hundred kilometres away. The Quarter took the current and refused the fixture. Silk over a lattice frame, filament inside — the same silhouette that hung here in the Ming, drawing off the same feed as the freight lanes overhead.

It took eleven years of hearings. The compromise, written into the district code and still posted at the head of Lantern Street, is one line long: the grid may decide what the light costs; the Quarter decides what it looks like.

The result is a district that is entirely modern and looks nothing like it. Forty-one thousand lanterns, each one addressable, each one made by hand within four streets of where it hangs.

五条街 · five streets

What is on the ground here

The Quarter is small enough to cross in twenty minutes and dense enough that nobody does it in under an hour.

01

Lantern Street

Main run · 900 m

The spine. Nine hundred metres of overhead line strung four deep, dense enough that the street has no true night — only a red one. The district code is nailed to a post at the north end, laminated, faded, never replaced.

02

Paper Row

Workshops · 112 registered

Where the frames are cut and the silk is stretched. A hundred and twelve workshops, most of them one room and one family. Apprenticeship is still seven years, which nobody has managed to shorten, because the last two are spent learning what to do when the silk is wrong.

03

The Filament Stair

Grid access · loop 7

A public stair with the district's junction boxes bolted along the wall, unhidden on purpose. Children are taught to read the load meters here before they are taught to hang a lantern. The stair smells of hot dust and wet stone in equal measure.

04

Wet Stone Market

Open 17:00 – tide

Named for the flagstones, which have not been dry since the falls were rerouted in ’68. Food, mostly. The reflection off the wet stone doubles the lantern count, and the Quarter counts it in the official figure, which the utility has stopped contesting.

05

The Hanging Alley

Memorial · unlit one night a year

Sixty metres wide at the top and two at the bottom, roofed entirely in lanterns. On the anniversary of the flood of ’61 it is the only street in 未来城 that goes dark, for one hour, by choice.
