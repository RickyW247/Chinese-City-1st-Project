The River Dragon

Not advertising. A civic projection, three hundred and forty metres of it, coiling above the exchange district since the year after the flood — rebuilt in fibre and fog, and still answering only to its old, unspoken name.

The projection

It has never been switched off, and no department will admit to owning the switch.

The source geometry is a scan of the stone dragon from the old river temple — the one carving that came through the flood of ’61 intact, because it was already underwater when the wall gave. The scan was meant as an archival record. Somebody in the reconstruction office put it on the emitters as a placeholder, on the night the exchange district came back online, and the placeholder was never replaced.

Six times since, a budget review has proposed decommissioning it. Six times the file has come back marked owner unresolved. The utility bills the projection to the district; the district books it as heritage lighting; the heritage office holds no record of it, on the grounds that a hologram is not a structure.

Twenty-six years of continuous broadcast. The pilots use it to judge cross-wind on the ridge approach. The market families steer by it. Nobody has been able to explain, in writing, why it is still there.

规格 · specification

What is actually up there

Filed once, in 2062, by an engineer who signed the form with a department that no longer exists.

01

Source geometry

Temple carving · scanned 2061

Nine hundred years of stone, captured at sub-millimetre and never re-modelled. Every scale in the sky is a scale someone cut by hand — including the chipped horn, which the reconstruction office has twice been asked to clean up and has twice declined.

02

Emitter ring

14 towers · exchange district

Fourteen rooftop emitters, phase-locked, throwing into the valley fog that sits over the river most nights. On the four or five clear nights a year the dragon is barely visible, which the city treats as weather rather than fault.

03

Motion

Never repeats

The coil is driven by river flow data — the actual gauge readings from Canal 7 and the falls, fed in live. It has therefore never made the same movement twice, and on the night of a release it visibly quickens.

04

Voice

Silent since installation

The emitter array includes an audio channel. It has never carried anything. The technicians who maintain it keep the amplifier powered anyway, and will not discuss why.

05

Name

Unrecorded

The temple's dragon had a name. It is not in the archive, it is not on the carving, and the families who would know it decline to write it down. Municipal documents refer to Projection 7, which nobody in the valley has ever said aloud.

播报日志 · broadcast log

Twenty-six years, six interruptions attempted

Extracted from the district maintenance record. The dragon has never gone dark; these are the times somebody tried.

2061 · 11 November

First light

The exchange district comes back on the grid eight months after the flood. The scan goes up on the emitters as a test pattern at 19:40. The test is not concluded.

2066 · Budget review

Decommission proposed (1 of 6)

Line item queried as “unattributed lighting load”. Referred to heritage. Returned marked not a structure. Load re-booked to the district and left there.

2071 · Emitter failure

Tower 9 drops out

A phase board fails during the winter storms. The coil thins along one flank for eleven days and does not break. Three separate crews replace the board without a work order, each believing another department had issued one.

2074 · Ownership audit

Owner unresolved

The city attempts to establish title in order to sell advertising rights along the dragon's length. No claimant is found — including the city. The proposal lapses and the file is closed without a recommendation.

2079 · Flood anniversary

The one dimming

At the request of the Lantern Quarter, output is reduced by half for one hour while the Hanging Alley goes dark. This is the only intentional change to the broadcast in its history, and it is repeated every year since.

2087 · Tonight

Still coiling

Fourteen emitters phase-locked, fog on the river, gauge data arriving from Canal 7 at one-second intervals. Status: nominal. Owner: unresolved.
