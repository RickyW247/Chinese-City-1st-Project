Manifesto

未来城 was never a city that arrived from the future. It is one that refused to leave the past behind — and dragged it, glowing, into tomorrow.

Somewhere between a temple bell and a server fan, its citizens found a version of the future that still remembers where the good tea is. Pagoda roofs sit on server farms. Sampans work the same canal they worked in the Ming, under bridges that now answer to air traffic control. The dragon over the skyline is a hologram, and it is still the oldest thing in the valley.
