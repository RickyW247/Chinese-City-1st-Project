造 · the three of us

The room it actually happened in

One desk, one borrowed monitor, three people arguing about a dragon. Everything on this site was made in this room, mostly at night, mostly while somebody was saying the coil looked wrong.

怎么做的 · how it was put together

What is actually running

No framework, no build step, nothing to install. Open any page in this project in a browser and it runs — which was the point.

01

The dragon

SVG · redrawn every frame

Not a video and not an image. The coil is a helix worked out in maths on every frame, cut into quads, sorted into ten depth bands and painted far band first so the body passes in front of itself. The head is a few dozen hand-written paths that mirror themselves rather than swim upside down.

02

The weather

Two canvases

Rain, glow dust, drifting wireframe solids and droplets that cling to the glass and then run down it — all particles, all drawn by hand into a canvas, all reacting to how fast you scroll. Scroll quickly and the dust lifts and brightens.

03

The type

Five faces, one grid

Unbounded for the headlines, Chakra Petch across the top bar, Ma Shan Zheng for the hanzi, Manrope for reading and JetBrains Mono for anything the city says about itself. Every heading carries its own dark halo so the dragon can pass behind it without eating the contrast.

04

The writing

Eight pages of it

Six quarters, an observation plate with eight annotated markers, a readout that counts itself up, and a ticker on every page. The city has dates, feuds, budget lines and at least one lost cat, and all of it had to agree with itself.

None of it is real. We built it anyway, and it took longer than the city would admit.
