Manifesto

未来城 was never a city that arrived from the future. It is one that refused to leave the past behind — and dragged it, glowing, into tomorrow.

Somewhere between a temple bell and a server fan, its citizens found a version of the future that still remembers where the good tea is. This is a working study in what a city keeps when it modernizes on its own terms, rather than the one handed to it by someone else's skyline.
